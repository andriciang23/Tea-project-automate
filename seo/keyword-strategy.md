Google Keyword Planner / Search Console once connected).

## On-page SEO rules (apply everywhere)

1. **SEO title (≤60 chars):** `Primary Keyword | Benefit | HojichaYa` — geo-modify head
   terms. Example: `Matcha Powder Malaysia — Uji Ceremonial | HojichaYa`.
2. **Meta description (150–160 chars):** keyword early + a reason to click (price from
   RMxx, low caffeine, free shipping over RMxxx, authentic Japanese). One clear CTA.
3. **H1 = product name** (kept natural/brand-led, geo-modifier optional).
4. **First 100 words of body** should contain the primary keyword once, naturally.
5. **Image alt text:** describe product + a keyword + "Malaysia".
6. **Internal links:** every product links to its collection + 1–2 related products
   (e.g. Aka ↔ Kyo, powders ↔ trio bundle).
7. **Structured data:** Shopify themes emit Product schema automatically — verify in
   Google's Rich Results Test after edits.
