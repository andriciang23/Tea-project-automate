# FAQ — explained super simply 🍵🤖

A tiny, friendly version of "how does this thing work?" — short words, no tech talk.

---

### 🤖 What is this?

It's a little robot helper that lives inside WhatsApp.

You text it a customer's order, and it writes that order down in your Shopify
shop for you. You can also ask it questions like "how much is the oolong?" and it
tells you.

It only ever talks to **you** — never your customers.

---

### 🧠 Does it think with Claude?

Yes! Claude is the robot's **brain**.

When you send a message, the robot shows it to Claude, Claude figures out what you
mean, and then the robot does it. So the smart part is Claude. 🧠✨

---

### 😴 Is it always awake?

Only if you keep it **plugged in**.

Think of the robot as living in a little house (a computer or a small server).
- If the house has its lights on (the program is running), the robot is awake and
  answers you. 💡
- If the house is dark (the program is stopped, or the computer is off), the robot
  is asleep and won't answer until you turn it back on. 🌙

So to have it answer **all day**, it needs to live somewhere that's always on — like
a cheap little cloud server — not your laptop that you close at night.

---

### 💵 Do I have to pay a subscription?

There's no single "subscription". Instead there are a few **small bills**, and most
have a free starting amount:

| What | What it's for | How you pay |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| 🧠 **Claude (Anthropic)** | The robot's thinking | A little bit each time it thinks. You add money to an account and it sips from it. |
| 💬 **WhatsApp (Meta)** | Sending/receiving messages | Meta gives a free amount; replying to you is usually free or very cheap. |
| 🏠 **A little server** | The robot's "house" so it stays awake | Often free or a few dollars a month. |
| 🛒 **Shopify** | Your shop | You already pay for this. Nothing new. |

So: **no flat subscription** — you mostly pay tiny amounts only when it's used. ☕

---

### 🛒 How does it know my prices and stock?

It **peeks at your Shopify shop live**, every single time.

It never uses an old list or a saved file. So the prices, stock, cost, and product
names it tells you are always the real, up-to-date ones. ✅

---

### 💳 Can the robot take my customers' money?

**No.** The robot can't charge anyone.

It just writes the order as a "draft" (like a sticky note). When your customer pays
you, you tell the robot "Jane paid", and **then** it marks that order as paid and
finishes it. You're always in charge of money. 🔒

---

### 📷 Can I send a photo of an order?

Yes! 📸

If a customer writes their order on paper, snap a photo and send it. The robot reads
the photo and turns it into an order.

🎤 **Voice notes don't work yet** — please type the order or send a photo instead.

---

### 🤔 What if the robot isn't sure?

It **asks you** instead of guessing.

For example, if you say "1 oolong" but oolong comes in 250g and 500g, it will ask
"which size?" It never makes up an answer. 🙋

---

### 🔐 Is it safe?

Yes, two ways:

1. It only listens to **your** phone number. If anyone else messages it, it ignores
   them. 🙉
2. Every message is checked with a **secret handshake** so fakes can't sneak in. 🤝

---

### 🆘 Something looks wrong — what do I do?

- The robot didn't reply? Its "house" might be asleep — check the program is running.
- It gave a weird answer? Just tell it again in simpler words. 🗣️
- Still stuck? Check the main `README.md` for the grown-up setup steps. 📖
