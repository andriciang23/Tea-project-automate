# Meta Ads plan (Facebook + Instagram)

Tea is visual and impulse-friendly — Meta (especially Instagram Reels/feed) is ideal for
discovery. Use the Shopify **Facebook & Instagram sales channel** so the product catalog,
Pixel, and Conversions API are wired automatically.

## Account structure

```
HojichaYa (Meta Ads)
├── Campaign: Advantage+ Shopping (ASC)        [prospecting+, RM12/day]
│   └── Single campaign, broad, AI-optimized, catalog sales
├── Campaign: Prospecting — Interest/Broad     [optional, RM0–8/day]
│   ├── Ad set: Broad (MY, 18–55) — let AI find buyers
│   └── Ad set: Interests — tea/matcha/wellness/Japanese food/cafés
└── Campaign: Retargeting — Catalog Sales      [RM8/day]
    ├── Ad set: Viewed/ATC last 14d → Dynamic Product Ads
    └── Ad set: IG/FB engagers + past customers (lookalike seed)
```

Meta now recommends consolidating into **Advantage+ Shopping** + a **retargeting**
campaign. Start there; add manual interest ad sets only if you want more control.

## Audiences

- **Prospecting:** Broad (Malaysia, 18–55, all genders) — Advantage+ finds buyers. Optional
  interests: *matcha, green tea, tea, Japanese cuisine, healthy eating, coffee, cafés,
  baking, wellness*.
- **Lookalikes:** 1–3% LAL of **purchasers** and of **add-to-cart** (build once you have
  ≥100 in the seed list).
- **Retargeting:** site visitors 14/30d, ATC not purchased, IG/FB video viewers (25%+),
  profile/post engagers, past customers (for new-product launches & bundles).

## Creative angles (make 4–6, refresh every 2–3 weeks)

1. **Education hook** — "Roasted green tea you can drink at night ☕ (it's low caffeine)" →
   Hojicha. Great for cold audiences.
2. **ASMR / process** — close-up whisking matcha or steeping hojicha (Reels).
3. **Latte at home** — recipe/how-to with the powder (Kaori / Kimidori) — saves & shares.
4. **Gifting** — bundles for Raya/CNY/year-end, "the tea-lover's gift".
5. **Social proof / UGC** — customer reviews, repost customer photos.
6. **Founder/origin story** — authentic Japanese tea, sourced & roasted, MY small business.

Formats: prioritize **Reels + 4:5 feed**. Always include captions (sound-off viewing).
Use the **catalog** for dynamic retargeting ads.

## Optimization

- Optimize for **Purchase** (use Conversions API for accuracy post-iOS).
- Give each ad set ~3–4 days / 50 conversions before judging (avoid early fiddling).
- Kill ads below ~1% CTR or with CPMs/CPA far above account average; scale winners by 20%.

## Setup checklist

- [ ] FB & IG sales channel connected in Shopify (catalog + Pixel + CAPI live)
- [ ] Domain verified + Aggregated Event Measurement configured (Purchase priority #1)
- [ ] Advantage+ Shopping campaign live with 4–6 creatives
- [ ] Retargeting campaign with Dynamic Product Ads
- [ ] UTM tracking template applied to all ads
