## Before you spend a cent

1. Connect Shopify's **Google** and **Facebook & Instagram** sales channels so your
   product catalog feeds both platforms (Settings → Apps and sales channels).
2. Install conversion tracking:
   - Meta: the **Meta Pixel + Conversions API** (auto-installed by the FB sales channel).
   - Google: **Google Ads conversion tag** + link **Merchant Center** (via the Google
     channel) for Shopping/Performance Max.
3. Make sure your SEO landing pages are polished (see [`/seo`](../seo)) — paid traffic
   converts far better on optimized product/collection pages.
4. Fill in credentials per [`docs/SETUP.md`](../docs/SETUP.md).

## Recommended starting budget

Small and measured for a Basic-plan shop. Suggested **RM30–50/day total** to start
(~RM900–1,500/month), split per [`strategy.md`](./strategy.md), then scale what works.
