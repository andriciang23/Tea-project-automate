# Paid strategy

## Objective

Profitable customer acquisition for HojichaYa, prioritizing first purchase of a
"gateway" product (Hojicha Aka, Genmaicha, Kimidori Matcha) then retention via email +
messaging (cross-pillar).

## Funnel & channel roles

| Stage | Audience | Channel | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Capture demand** | People searching "matcha/hojicha malaysia" | Google Search + Shopping/PMax | Catch high intent |
| **Create demand** | Tea/wellness/foodie interests in MY | Meta Advantage+ (prospecting) | Discovery, cheap reach |
| **Retarget** | Site visitors, add-to-cart, IG/FB engagers | Meta retargeting + Google PMax | Recover abandoners |
| **Retain** | Past customers | Email + WhatsApp/IG broadcast (free) | Repeat orders |

## Budget split (starting ~RM40/day)

| Channel | Daily | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Search/PMax | RM20 | Highest-intent buyers searching for your products |
| Meta prospecting | RM12 | Cheap discovery; tea is visual & impulse-friendly on IG |
| Meta retargeting | RM8 | High ROAS; recovers warm traffic |

Reassess after 2 weeks. Shift budget toward whichever has the lower **cost per purchase**.

## Targets (first-90-days, directional)

- Blended **ROAS ≥ 2.5–3x** once optimized (tea has good repeat value, so allow lower ROAS
  on first order if retention is strong).
- Target **cost per purchase ≤ ~RM25–35** for gateway products.
- Build a retargeting pool of **≥1,000** site visitors before leaning hard on retargeting.

## Geo & seasonality

- **Geo:** Malaysia, with bid emphasis on Klang Valley, Penang, Johor (higher e-comm
  density). Consider Singapore later if you ship there.
- **Peaks:** Ramadan/Raya gifting, year-end holidays, CNY (Obukucha/gift bundles), and any
  matcha-trend spikes. Pre-build gift-bundle campaigns for these.

## Measurement

- Single source of truth: **Shopify Analytics + UTM tags** on every ad.
- Watch platform-reported ROAS *and* Shopify's actual orders — they'll differ; trust
  Shopify for spend decisions.
- See [`automation.md`](./automation.md) for automated weekly reporting.
